## Product Principles

1. Real numbers over adjectives: show live ratings and shipped projects, never inflate them.
2. The first ten seconds answer who, what role, and where to click (projects, resume, contact).
3. Every project can be verified: live URL and client repo on every detail page.
4. Modest numbers are presented honestly and with context (growth over time), not hidden.
